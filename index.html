About

I work on Askden Ghana — a multi-channel platform for cast concrete, fibreglass, and related products — using Medusa 2 and Next.js for B2C and B2B, with payments, credit, and warehouse workflows.

At Voltic (Golden Rains LTD.) — a Voltic Cool Pac franchise producing certified bagged drinking water — I apply embedded systems engineering alongside production and operational technology: interfacing sensors and controllers, reliable firmware, and practical automation on the line.

I also publish open, book-style guides on GitHub for solo engineers who want stronger project breakdown and DevOps/DevSecOps practice without gatekeeping jargon.
